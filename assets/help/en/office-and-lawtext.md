# Office Documents, Lawtext & Structured Data

[[help/index|← Back to Help Index]]

## 1. Office-Compatible Formats

- **Word Processing (`.docx`, `.odt`, `.rtf`, `.wpd`)**: View and edit body text, tables, shapes, footnotes, comments, and tracked changes, with PDF export.
- **Spreadsheets (`.xlsx`, `.ods`, `.csv`, `.tsv`)**: Multi-sheet workbooks, formula recalculation, charts, and conditional formatting.
- **Presentations (`.pptx`, `.odp`)**: Slide editing, animations, 3D transitions, and slideshow playback.
- **Ichitaro (`.jtd`)**: View vertical/table documents locally and convert to `.docx` or HTML.
- **DocuWorks (`.xdw`) & Visio (`.vsdx`)**: View pages and multi-page diagrams.

## 2. Lawtext & e-Gov Legal XML (`.lawtext`, `.xml`)

Supports bidirectional Split editing between **Lawtext** and **e-Gov Legal Standard XML**:

- Structured article/paragraph/item preview
- Live legislative drafting lint checks

## 3. Structured Data Viewers

- **AI Evaluation Logs (`.jsonl`)**: Inspect prompts, model outputs, eval scores, and Pass/Fail status in a structured table.
- **CycloneDX SBOM (`.cdx.json`)**: Browse software bills of materials in spreadsheet view.
- **Wide / Repeating-Column CSV**: Expand and search repeating column groups in a structured tree view.
